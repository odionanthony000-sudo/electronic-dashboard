A business doesn't need more data. It needs to understand what the data is saying.

For this project, I worked with raw sales records from an electronics business and used Microsoft Excel to transform the data into an interactive Sales & Profit Performance Dashboard.

🛠️ Tools & techniques used
Microsoft Excel
Excel Tables
PivotTables
PivotCharts
KPI calculations
Data aggregation and analysis
Interactive filters/slicers
Data visualization
🎯 The business problem

The company had sales records containing information such as:

Date | City | Customer Type | Product | Category | Cost Price | Quantity Sold | Selling Price | COGS | Revenue | Profit | Payment Method

But raw rows of transactions don't immediately tell management:

Which products are performing best?
Which locations generate the most revenue?
Where is profit coming from?
Which customer type contributes more?
How are sales changing over time?

💡 My solution

I transformed the raw records into an interactive dashboard that allows management to quickly monitor:

📌 Total Revenue
📌 Total Profit
📌 Average Order Value
📌 Total Quantity Sold
📌 Total Orders
📌 Monthly Revenue & Profit Performance
📌 Product Performance
📌 Category Performance
📌 City Performance
📌 Payment Channel Performance
📌 Membership Performance

I also added interactive filters for Date, Customer Type, City and Category, allowing users to drill down into the data instead of relying on a static report.

📈 The result

The dashboard turns a large collection of transaction records into a management-friendly view of sales performance and profitability.

Instead of asking:

“What happened?”

the company can start asking:

“Why did it happen, where did it happen, and what should we do next?”
